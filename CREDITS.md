# Credits

## Art, music, effects and jingles

**Ninja Adventure Asset Pack** by [Pixel-boy](https://pixel-boy.itch.io/) and [AAA](https://www.instagram.com/challenger.aaa/), released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): https://pixel-boy.itch.io/ninja-adventure-asset-pack

Credit isn't required, but it's gratefully given. The game uses:
- the tilesets, including the desert set (market stalls and palms) and the boat and sail;
- the character, animal, monster and boss sprite sheets, and the portraits;
- the two dragons, which come as separate parts (head, body rings, tail and wing) and are put together in code;
- the battle effect sheets: explosions, fire, ice, lightning, water pillars, rock spikes, leaves, smoke, sparks, shields and slashes;
- the item icons, from food and potions to scrolls, gems and weapons;
- thirty-seven of the music tracks and a few jingles.

They are copied into `assets/` under shorter names, and `assets/LICENSE-NinjaAdventure.txt` is the pack's own licence.

Some things are drawn and synthesized in code for this game:
- the peach on the title screen and in cutscenes;
- the windows and the battle backdrops;
- the coral, lava, currents and fog;
- the fishing and mochi-pounding screens;
- the sound effects.

## Font

Cubic 11 (俐方體11號). It is free to use and redistribute; see `fonts/Cubic_11-LICENSE.txt`.

## Story

The story and dialogue are original. The outline comes from the traditional, public-domain Japanese folk tale of the boy born from a peach.
